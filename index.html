Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every template, every feature and every download is free. There's no trial, no paywall at the download button, and no watermark.

Where is my resume stored?

Only in your browser, on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. To move to another computer, or to keep a safe copy, use Backup to download a file you can import later.

Will it get through applicant tracking systems?

All five templates use a single column of real, selectable text with standard section headings, the format applicant tracking systems read most reliably. There are no images, tables or text boxes hiding your words.

How do I get the PDF?

Click Download PDF. Your browser's print window opens with the page size and margins already set; choose Save as PDF. The result is a sharp vector file, not a screenshot.

Can I import my LinkedIn profile?

Yes, two ways. The quickest: on your profile, choose Resources › Save to PDF and import that file. The most accurate: request LinkedIn's data export and import the ZIP when it arrives.

Does it work on my phone?

It does. Switch between editing and the live preview with one tap. For long editing sessions, a larger screen is more comfortable.
